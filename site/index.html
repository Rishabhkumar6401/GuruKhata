Common questions

What is a fees book / fees register app?

It is the digital version of the paper register tuition and coaching teachers use to note students' monthly fees. A fees register app records each student's fee, due date and payment, and additionally reminds parents on WhatsApp and totals your pending fees automatically.

Is GuruKhata free?

Yes — the pilot is completely free. Later, the core fees book will stay free and advanced features (automatic reminders, full history) will cost less than a single student's one-month fee.

Does the money go through GuruKhata?

No, never. Parents pay you directly to your own UPI. GuruKhata only keeps your register and sends reminders — your money never touches us.

I teach multiple batches. Does it work batch-wise?

Yes — GuruKhata is built for coaching classes with batches of 30–60 students, as well as home tutors with 10.
